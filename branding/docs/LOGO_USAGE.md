# Logo and icon usage

The approved logo is the **chamfered outer portal + copper F + small rising gateway wedge**. It is not an arch with battlements, a shield, or the original prototype logo.

## Preferred variants

| Context | Use |
| --- | --- |
| White / Paper background | `../assets/svg/marks/ferrowarden-mark-light.svg` |
| Iron / Deep Iron background | `../assets/svg/marks/ferrowarden-mark-dark.svg` |
| Light layout with brand name | `../assets/svg/lockups/ferrowarden-lockup-light-tagline.svg` |
| Dark layout with brand name | `../assets/svg/lockups/ferrowarden-lockup-dark-tagline.svg` |
| Icon sized 16–32 px | Pre-rendered favicon tile or `favicon.ico` |
| Black-and-white printing | Monochrome Iron or Paper SVG |

## Anatomy and spacing

- Outer portal: one continuous steel piece, beveled diagonally at its upper left and rounded on the other top corner.
- F: copper in full-color applications, with the same notch and angled terminal as the source master.
- Gateway wedge: a distinct shape to the F's lower right, Iron for light, Paper for dark.
- Do not merge the F and wedge, introduce an extra inset box, or extend the portal into a full square.
- Leave at least 10% icon-width clear space around the mark and 5% of a banner width at the outer margins.
- Lockup with tagline should be at least 600 px wide for comfortable raster use. Below this, use the no-tagline variant. At 24–32 px, prefer the standalone symbol.

## Correct usage

- Keep the exact three-path master geometry and scale components together.
- Use the approved colors and correct light/dark gateway color.
- Keep sufficient color contrast against the background.
- Keep the tagline in sentence case: “The credential boundary for AI agents”.
- Maintain the same wordmark case: **Ferrowarden**.

## Incorrect usage

- Do not use earlier generated concepts, particularly the battlemented versions.
- Do not redraw the icon as a padlock, castle door, shield, keyhole or nested frame.
- Do not invert the *F* to black/white in the full-color version. It stays copper.
- Do not distort, skew, rotate, stretch or apply strong decorative shadows.
- Do not attach the Vaultwarden or Bitwarden emblems as part of the Ferrowarden mark.
- Do not place the wordmark over complicated visual texture or with insufficient contrast.

## Accessibility and alt text

For decorative icons inside a labeled control, use `alt=""` or mark the SVG as decorative. For meaningful standalone brand marks, `alt="Ferrowarden"` is appropriate. The banner needs no duplicate alt text if a visible page heading already says “Ferrowarden”; otherwise `alt="Ferrowarden — The credential boundary for AI agents"` is suitable.

## One-color use

The mono versions are for stamps, photocopying, neutral buttons, and restrictive contexts. The negative-space channels still distinguish outer portal, F and wedge. Use the standard copper identity everywhere normal color rendering is available.
